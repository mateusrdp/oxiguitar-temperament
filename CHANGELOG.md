### Added

- **The temperament plugin** (mateusrdp/oxiguitar#294): a fretted track's section **Temperament** in the track dialog chooses how its notes are tuned. The choices are **Off (equal temperament)**; **Realistic guitar**, where fretted notes go sharp as a real guitar's do from string stretching and stiffness, on a nylon, steel, electric or bass guitar set up with its 12th fret in tune; **True Temperament: Thidell Formula 1**; and **True Temperament: equal temperament**. Bends, slides and vibrato keep their shape. Needs plugin interface 1.12.
